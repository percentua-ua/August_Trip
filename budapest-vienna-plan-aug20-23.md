# Budapest + Vienna — 3 adults, by car from Rivne, Aug 20–24, 2026

**Thu Aug 20** leave Rivne 05:00, in Budapest ~15:00, evening = the national-holiday show · **Fri Aug 21** full day Budapest · **Sat Aug 22** drive to Vienna + half day · **Sun Aug 23** full day Vienna · **Mon Aug 24** leave Vienna 06:00, home the same night.

**Priority is seeing the cities** — walking, the main sights, viewpoints. Everything below is built to walk in a line, with the hottest hours indoors.

### The one thing that shapes everything

**Aug 20 is Hungary's national day (Szent István-nap)** — free programme across ten sites, a 21:00 show on the Danube, shops shut, embankments closed to cars, huge crowds. **Aug 21 is a transferred rest day**, so Hungary has a four-day weekend and Budapest is at peak domestic-tourism load the whole time you're there. Vienna by contrast is calm — but **Sunday Aug 23 every shop in Austria is closed** (restaurants, cafés and museums stay open).

## MAIN ATTRACTIONS — the short list

**Budapest, in order of what you'd regret missing**

1. **Parliament interior** — Dome Hall, Grand Staircase, the Holy Crown. Free open day Friday, no booking.
2. **Castle Hill** — Fisherman's Bastion, Matthias Church, Trinity Square, the Castle terraces.
3. **The river itself** — Liberty Bridge, the car-free quays, the Chain Bridge on foot, Parliament lit up at night.
4. **Gellért Hill / Citadella** — the one panorama that contains the whole city.
5. **The Great Market Hall** — across your street, and best at 07:45.
6. **Shoes on the Danube Bank** — small, quiet, the most affecting thing in the city.
7. **St Stephen's Basilica dome terrace** — best central viewpoint in Pest.
8. **Heroes' Square + City Park** with **Vajdahunyad Castle**, reached on the **M1**, the oldest metro on the continent after London.
9. **Dohány Street Synagogue** (largest in Europe) or the **House of Terror** — the indoor hour.
10. **District VII ruin bars** — the evening.

**Vienna**

1. **Schönbrunn + the Gloriette** — go at 08:00, the gardens and the walk up are free.
2. **Stephansdom** and the **Innere Stadt** loop: Karlsplatz, Staatsoper, Kärntner Straße, Graben, Hofburg.
3. **Kunsthistorisches Museum** (open Sun, closed Mon) or the **Belvedere** for Klimt's *The Kiss*.
4. **Neustifter Kirtag** — the 300-year-old wine-village festival, UNESCO-listed rituals, and it happens to run exactly on your dates. The single best thing available to you in Vienna.
5. **Rathausplatz film festival** — free, both nights, food stalls till midnight.
6. **A proper coffee house** — Sperl or Central, sat down, no hurry.
7. **Naschmarkt** on Saturday, when the flea market runs.

## THU AUG 20 — arrival + the national holiday show

**In Budapest 15:00–15:30 after eleven hours on the road.** The priority tonight is the 21:00 show, not squeezing in sights. Parliament is Friday's job. **Take a real hour flat on a bed** — it'll do more for the evening than an extra walk.

**Why the route goes this way:** the evening's food street, the *Magyar Ízek Utcája*, is at **Várkert Bazár on the Buda side** — not by Parliament — and the **Buda lower quay is closed to cars Aug 15–22**. So you cross Liberty Bridge once and walk north up a car-free riverside promenade all evening, ending opposite Parliament. **3.6 km, ~44 min of walking, no backtracking.**

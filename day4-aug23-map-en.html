Day 4 — Sunday 23 August

Austria is shut, so the day is built around what isn't. Out of the flat at 07:00, something to eat at Meidling station — the only thing open — then Schönbrunn while it's empty while it's empty, market breakfast at 09:45, a free jazz brunch, the museum that's only open today — and then out to the Neustifter Kirtag on its free last night — and straight home from there, because Monday starts at 05:20.

Breakfast on a closed Sunday: Meidlinger Markt, 200 m away. No hotel breakfast, and no shop in Austria is open — but Ignaz & Rosalia, the market's coffee house, opens 09:00 on Sundays and serves breakfast to 12:00. Order the Marktfrühstück: baguette, stuffed vine leaves, sheep's cheese, hard cheese, Croatian house sausage, hummus, olives. Or the classic Viennese — coffee, soft-boiled egg, bread, butter, jam. ~€35–50 for the three of you. Market stalls are shut; the cafés are open 10:00–18:00. Meidlinger Alm in the same market is the backup from 10:00.
Reading the map, and lighting up one leg at a time. Dashed = you walk, and each walking leg has its own colour so consecutive legs never blur into one another — the badge on the line matches its colour and names the two stops it joins. Solid = you ride, in the transport line's real colour — and now badged exactly the same way: the line, and the two stops that leg joins.
Click any line and it lights up on its own — everything else dims, the map zooms to it, and a bar appears top-left with a show the whole day button. Click the same line again to undo it.
Click a numbered stop in the list and every leg touching that stop lights up — so clicking 11 shows you how you arrive at 11 and how you leave it, together.
Eating on a Sunday, in order. 07:00 you leave the flat and nothing in Austria is open — Ignaz & Rosalia doesn't unlock until 09:00.
07:15 first food: Bahnhof Wien Meidling station shops, 630 m south. Station shops are exempt from Sunday closing, so this is the only reliable coffee and pastry near you at that hour — and Meidling is a U6 stop, so it costs you nothing on the way to Schönbrunn.
Or skip it: if you bought bread, fruit and coffee on Saturday as planned, eat at the flat and use Niederhofstraße instead.
09:45 the proper breakfast: Ignaz & Rosalia on Meidlinger Markt, 200 m from your bed, open 09:00–18:00 on Sundays. Order the Marktfrühstück. That is the meal — the station is just fuel for the Gloriette.
Everything retail is closed today, and it's absolute. No supermarkets, no Trafiks, no market stalls. Restaurants, cafés, museums and the Rathausplatz stalls are open. If you didn't buy water and food for tomorrow's 1,000 km drive on Saturday, the station shops at Bahnhof Wien Meidling (630 m) are your only realistic option — station shops are exempt from Sunday closing.
Tonight is the Neustifter Kirtag's last night, and Sunday is its free day. 12:00–22:00, no table reservations sold at all, brass band from 12:00 and Die Wuppa 17:00–21:00. A 650 m stretch of wine-village road closed to traffic, Heuriger tables in the street, running since the mid-1700s, UNESCO-listed rituals, 100,000 visitors across four days. Arriving at 18:00 catches the best of it. Don't drive — U6 to Nussdorfer Straße then bus 35A, both on your ticket.
Schönbrunn before 10:00 or not at all. The gardens and the walk up to the Gloriette are free, you're two U4 stops away, and by 11:00 it's the busiest site in Austria. This is the one morning of the trip where an early alarm buys you something real. The palace interior needs a booked slot and ~€32 — skip it without regret.
Museums: today or never. The Kunsthistorisches is open Sunday and closed Monday, and a shower is forecast this afternoon — hence the 14:00 indoor block. Largest Bruegel collection in the world, plus Vermeer, Velázquez and the Kunstkammer. Allow 2–3 h. The Belvedere with Klimt's The Kiss is the smaller, cheaper, faster alternative.
⚠ You drive 1,000 km tomorrow, starting at 06:00. Austria is 0.5 ‰; Hungary is 0.00 ‰ and you cross it for 530 km. Whoever drives first is on spritzers without the wine tonight. Pack the car, agree the rotation and get anything off the roof before the Kirtag, not after.
